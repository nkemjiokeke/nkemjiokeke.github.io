About

Systems thinking, across sectors

I work across industries because business problems don't respect industry boundaries — and neither does good analysis. My approach is grounded in systems thinking: find the real constraint, quantify it precisely, and give decision-makers a clear path forward.

My focus is on sectors where analysis carries real operational and financial weight — energy and petrochemicals, utilities, freight and logistics, insurance, development finance, and public health — with a particular edge in the industrial corridor of Sarnia–Lambton, Ontario and the markets of West Africa.

Based in Sarnia, Ontario. Open to remote data and business analyst roles globally.
